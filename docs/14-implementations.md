# 14. Popular Anticheat Implementations Overview

The ecosystem is large and constantly shifting; the following characterizations reflect publicly discussed strengths around the mid-2020s:

- **GrimAC**: Open-source and prediction-centric. Its detailed movement simulation is widely regarded as among the strongest free options. Often paired with separate combat-focused plugins.
- **Vulcan**: Commercial, strong packet-level analysis, balanced movement and combat coverage, generally low false-positive reputation when configured appropriately.
- **Matrix**: Commercial, broad check set, historically popular in multiple regions, includes a variety of combat and movement heuristics.
- **NoCheatPlus and maintained forks**: One of the longest-lived open-source projects; still used, though newer predictive engines have surpassed it in movement accuracy.
- **Spartan, Polar, Intave, Verus**, and many others: Each emphasizes different combinations of combat heuristics, performance characteristics, Bedrock support, or update frequency.
- Specialized combat layers (various commercial and free projects) that deliberately avoid duplicating movement simulation and instead focus on aim, click, and aura patterns, often designed to run alongside GrimAC or similar.

Selection criteria for a given server include game mode (casual SMP versus competitive PvP), budget, supported Minecraft versions, expected player count, tolerance for configuration work, and whether Bedrock/Geyser players must be supported.
