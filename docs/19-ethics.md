# 19. Ethical and Legal Notes

This document exists to explain defensive technology so that server operators, plugin developers, and researchers can build and maintain fairer multiplayer environments. It deliberately avoids any guidance that would help a reader construct, obtain, or operate cheats.

Using modified clients to gain unfair advantages on servers that prohibit them generally violates the server's terms of service. Consequences can include account suspension, hardware identifiers being blocked, and, depending on jurisdiction and circumstances, legal exposure under computer-access or terms-of-service statutes. Server operators should publish clear rules, apply them consistently, and ensure that technical measures are proportionate to the harms they address.
